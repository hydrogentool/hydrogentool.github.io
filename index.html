What Is Hydrogen Executor?

Hydrogen Executor is a multi-platform Roblox script executor that supports Windows, macOS, and Android. It is one of the few executors that offers a native macOS version, making it a notable option for users outside the typical Windows ecosystem.

According to recent community sources, Hydrogen supports Level 8 Lua execution with approximately 96% sUNC compatibility. It also includes integration for the require() function, which allows scripts to load ModuleScripts directly by asset ID — a feature not available in many competing executors.

Hydrogen is actively maintained with regular updates. The executor is distributed through its official website and community channels. It includes a built-in script hub, editor, and support for most community scripts. Mobile versions for Android are also available, though they may require additional setup steps.
